Open CASCADE Technology (OCCT) — Third-Party Notice
====================================================

This SAM installation includes binary components of Open CASCADE Technology
(OCCT) version 8.0.0, used by the SAM_OCCT geometry engine
(SAM.Occt.Native.dll) through dynamic linking.

The OCCT runtime libraries distributed with SAM include (non-exhaustive):
the OCCT modeling, mesh, data-exchange and visualization toolkits
(TK*.dll) and the third-party runtime they require (e.g. freetype.dll,
FreeImage.dll). These files are installed alongside the SAM assemblies in:

    %APPDATA%\SAM

Replacing the OCCT binaries
---------------------------
Because OCCT is dynamically linked, you may replace the OCCT runtime DLLs in
%APPDATA%\SAM with your own build of the same OCCT 8.0.0 (or a compatible,
modified version), provided the exported toolkit interfaces are preserved.
SAM loads these DLLs from %APPDATA%\SAM at runtime; replacing them there is
sufficient and does not require relinking SAM.

Third-party runtime bundled with OCCT
-------------------------------------
The OCCT runtime ships alongside third-party libraries (e.g. FreeType,
FreeImage, Qt, VTK, Intel TBB). Their individual licence and notice files are
installed, preserving each package's folder, under:

    %APPDATA%\SAM\licenses\3rdparty

Refer to those files for the terms governing each non-OCCT component.
